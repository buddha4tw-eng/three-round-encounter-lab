Calculation method & assumptions

Attack rolls

Uses d20 hit probability against target AC, natural 1/20 rules, optional advantage/disadvantage, and extra critical-hit damage dice. Expected damage is the sum of outcome damage × probability.

Saving throws

Uses save DC versus the representative target save bonus. Choose half damage on a successful save or no damage on a success. Area effects multiply per-target expected damage by the expected number of targets.

Round schedule

Choose which rounds each attack is used. For recharge abilities, set the expected number of uses by round manually. This makes assumptions explicit instead of falsely treating a recharge power as available every turn.

Monster encounter model

Monster attacks use the same engine as character attacks, but target party AC/saves. Multiattack is represented by adding each attack separately. Add legendary actions as separate attack entries in the rounds they are expected to occur.
